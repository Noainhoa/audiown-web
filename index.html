ListenEasy

An offline audiobook player for your own local audio files on iPhone and iPad.

Privacy Policy Support

Key Features

  • Import your own audio files and folders.
  • Open audio files from Files, AirDrop, and Share.
  • Resume playback with saved progress for your books.
  • Add bookmarks while listening.
  • Use chapters or multi-part navigation when available.
  • Adjust playback speed and set a sleep timer.
  • Control playback from the lock screen and Control Center.

Local First

ListenEasy works with audio files you choose from your device. It does not require an account, does not use ads, does not track you, and does not upload your audio files.

Your library references, playback progress, bookmarks, and settings are stored locally on your device.
