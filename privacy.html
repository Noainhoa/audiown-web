Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

OneListen is an offline audiobook player for audio files you provide on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

OneListen does not collect personal data. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server for your listening activity.

Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

OneListen does not track users. OneListen does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party advertising.

Audio Files

OneListen does not upload your audio files. Imported audio files remain on your device and are accessed only so the app can play the files you choose.

Information Stored on Your Device

OneListen stores local app data on your device, including:

  • Imported library references, such as file and folder references.
  • Playback progress and playback speed.
  • Bookmarks.
  • Settings, such as skip intervals and playback preferences.

This data is stored locally by the app and is removed according to normal iOS app deletion and backup behavior.

Support Emails

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will be used only to respond to your request.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. The last updated date will change when the policy changes.
