Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

ListenEasy is an offline audiobook player for audio files you provide on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

ListenEasy does not collect personal data. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server for your listening activity.

Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

ListenEasy does not track users. ListenEasy does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party advertising.

Audio Files

ListenEasy does not upload your audio files. Imported audio files remain on your device and are accessed only so the app can play the files you choose, including files opened with Open with ListenEasy.

Optional Cover Art Lookup

If you enable Automatic cover lookup in Settings, ListenEasy sends only the book title and author to Apple Books and Open Library to search for cover images. Your audio files are not sent. This feature is off by default.

Microphone and Speech (optional note dictation)

If you use Dictate while editing a note, ListenEasy requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. Speech is converted to text for that note on your device via Apple’s speech APIs. ListenEasy does not upload dictation audio to ListenEasy servers. You can deny permission and still type notes manually.

Information Stored on Your Device

ListenEasy stores local app data on your device, including:

  • Imported library references, such as file and folder references.
  • Playback progress and playback speed.
  • Notes and bookmarks.
  • Settings, such as skip intervals and playback preferences.

This data is stored locally by the app and is removed according to normal iOS app deletion and backup behavior.

Support Emails

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will be used only to respond to your request.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. The last updated date will change when the policy changes.
